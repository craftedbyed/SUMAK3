Gravity Cascade Cross-Section

Fish-only cascade from fish tank through primary biofilter and secondary biofilter (media bed) to sump, with a single low-duty lift pump returning water to the fish tank.

  • Cascade Path: Fish Tank → Primary Biofilter → Secondary Biofilter (Media Bed) → Sump.
  • Passive Aeration: Water falling between tiers provides passive aeration.
  • Return Pump: Single low-duty lift pump returns water from sump to fish tank.

Overall System Architecture

Sensors feed the ESP32 controller, logbook, and web dashboard, driving actuators and interfacing with the cascade and power block.

  • Sensors: Temperature, flow, level, ultrasonic, and vibration / electret microphone.
  • ESP32 & Telemetry: Controller, oxygen-risk proxy (Proxy, not a DO sensor), logbook, and web dashboard.
  • Actuators & Power: Lift pump, relays, feeder (servo or stepper), alarm LEDs, and power block.

Water & Measured-Reuse Cycle

Water circulates sump → pump → fish tank → cascade → sump with measured reuse, top-up metering, and protection trips.

  • Circulation Loop: Sump → Lift Pump → Fish Tank → Gravity Cascade → Sump.
  • Reuse & Top-Up: Level and flow sensors measure water reuse and top-up.
  • Protection Trips: Leak and dry-run protection trips safeguard operation.

Oxygen-Risk Guardian Decision Loop

Temperature, night period, time since feeding, and flow feed the proxy score (Proxy, not a DO sensor), validated offline against the JKUAT laboratory DO meter.

  • Proxy Inputs: Temperature, night period, time since feeding, and flow.
  • Actions & Logging: Raises circulation, triggers alarms, and logs results.
  • Offline Validation: Validated offline against the JKUAT laboratory DO meter.

Feed-Response Feeder & FCR Loop

Vibration or electret-microphone activity index drives servo or stepper feed pulses with a timed fallback, logging feed amounts and growth for FCR.

  • Actuation & Fallback: Activity index → servo/stepper pulse → feed delivered, with timed fallback.
  • FCR Logging: Feed amounts and growth feed the FCR log.

Power Architecture & Outage Survival

Solar input and Li-ion cells supply the lift pump, ESP32, sensors, and other loads, with power-cut survival time recorded as a measurement.

  • Supply & Loads: Solar input and Li-ion cells power the lift pump, ESP32 & sensors, and other loads.
  • Outage Survival: Runs on Li-ion cells during power cuts with survival time recorded as a measurement.
